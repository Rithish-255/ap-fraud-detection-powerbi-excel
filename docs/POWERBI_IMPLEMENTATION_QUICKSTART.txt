=== SECTION 1: PREREQUISITES AND SETUP ===

=== SECTION 3: DATA MODEL DESIGN ===

3.1 Create Dimension Tables

Vendor Dimension:
- New Table > DISTINCT(AP_Transactions[Vendor_ID])

Department Dimension:
- New Table > DISTINCT(AP_Transactions[Department])

Approval Status Dimension:
- New Table > DISTINCT(AP_Transactions[Approval_Status])

Payment Mode Dimension:
- New Table > DISTINCT(AP_Transactions[Payment_Mode])

3.2 Create Relationships
- AP_Transactions[Vendor_ID] → Vendor_Dimension[Vendor_ID]
- AP_Transactions[Department] → Department_Dimension[Department]
- AP_Transactions[Approval_Status] → Approval_Status_Dimension[Approval_Status]
- AP_Transactions[Payment_Mode] → Payment_Mode_Dimension[Payment_Mode]

3.3 Mark as Date Table
- Right-click AP_Transactions > Mark as Date Table
- Column: Invoice_Date

=== SECTION 4: DAX MEASURES ===

4.1 Basic Measures

Total Invoices:
Total Invoices = DISTINCTCOUNT(AP_Transactions[Invoice_ID])

Total Amount:
Total Amount = SUM(AP_Transactions[Amount])

Total Vendors:
Total Vendors = DISTINCTCOUNT(AP_Transactions[Vendor_ID])

4.2 Duplicate Payment Measures

Duplicate Payment Count:
Duplicate Payment Count = CALCULATE(COUNTROWS(AP_Transactions), AP_Transactions[Duplicate_Flag] = "Yes")

Duplicate Payment Amount:
Duplicate Payment Amount = CALCULATE(SUM(AP_Transactions[Amount]), AP_Transactions[Duplicate_Flag] = "Yes")

4.3 Rogue Payment Measures

Rogue Payment Count:
Rogue Payment Count = CALCULATE(COUNTROWS(AP_Transactions), AP_Transactions[Rogue_Payment_Flag] = "Yes")

Rogue Payment Amount:
Rogue Payment Amount = CALCULATE(SUM(AP_Transactions[Amount]), AP_Transactions[Rogue_Payment_Flag] = "Yes")

4.4 Approval Measures

Approval Bypass Count:
Approval Bypass Count = CALCULATE(COUNTROWS(AP_Transactions), OR(AP_Transactions[Approved_By] = "NA", ISBLANK(AP_Transactions[Approved_By])))

Approved Invoices:
Approved Invoices = CALCULATE(COUNTROWS(AP_Transactions), AP_Transactions[Approval_Status] = "Approved")

4.5 Timing Measures

Avg Days To Pay:
Avg Days To Pay = AVERAGE(AP_Transactions[Days_To_Pay])

Delayed Payments:
Delayed Payments = CALCULATE(COUNTROWS(AP_Transactions), AP_Transactions[Days_To_Pay] > 15)

4.6 Risk Measures

Avg Risk Score:
Avg Risk Score = AVERAGE(AP_Transactions[Risk_Score])

High Risk Invoices:
High Risk Invoices = CALCULATE(COUNTROWS(AP_Transactions), AP_Transactions[Risk_Score] >= 75)

=== EXPECTED RESULTS ===

Duplicate Payments: 2-5 cases identified
Rogue Payments: 1-3 cases identified
Approval Bypass: 3-5 cases flagged
High Risk Vendors: 2-3 vendors requiring review
Average Days to Pay: 12-15 days
Exception Rate: 3-7% of total invoices

=== BUSINESS INSIGHTS ===

1. Duplicate payments cluster around specific vendors
2. Rogue payments often indicate approval control gaps
3. AP and Finance departments show higher exception rates
4. Payment delays correlate with higher risk scores
5. Certain payment modes (Wire) show higher rogue payment risk

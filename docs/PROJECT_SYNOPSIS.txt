# Accounts Payable & Duplicate Payment Detection using Business Analytics and Power BI

## Project Synopsis

### Title
Accounts Payable & Duplicate Payment Detection using Business Analytics and Power BI

### Objective
To build a business analytics model for detecting duplicate and rogue payments in the accounts payable workflow using Excel and Power BI dashboards.

### Problem Statement
Organizations often face financial leakage in the accounts payable process due to duplicate invoice payments, approval bypass, delayed payments, and vendor anomaly patterns. Manual review is slow and inefficient, and many suspicious transactions remain hidden without a structured exception monitoring process.

### Scope
This project focuses on analyzing a synthetic dataset of accounts payable transactions and applying business rules to detect fraudulent or suspicious payment patterns. It demonstrates how Excel and Power BI can be used to convert transaction data into actionable management insights.

### Business Rules
1. Duplicate Payment Rule:
   If the same Vendor_ID, Amount, and Payment_Reference appear more than once, the record is marked as duplicate.

2. Rogue Payment Rule:
   If the payment is made with rejected approval, missing approval, or payment before approval, it is marked as rogue.

3. Approval Bypass Rule:
   If Approved_By is blank or NA, or approval is missing, the transaction is flagged for investigation.

4. Delay Rule:
   If Days_To_Pay exceeds the defined threshold, the payment is considered delayed and higher risk.

The dashboard is expected to show:
- duplicate payments on same vendor and payment reference
- rogue payments released without valid approval
- repeated high-risk vendors such as Vendor_08
- delayed payments in AP and Finance departments
- risk clusters requiring review before next payment cycle

### Example Output Summary

- Rogue payments: 1+ unauthorized cases

- Delay risk: above threshold in multiple transactions
- Control gaps: missing or rejected approval trail
